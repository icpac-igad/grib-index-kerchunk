# Daily runs, 2026-10-01 → 2026-10-15

What the daily ECMWF ENS → realized EA/SW-IO routine actually did over its
first fifteen operational days on EWC. Every run is **00z, era 50r1**. Machine-
readable copy: [`daily_runs_2026-10.csv`](daily_runs_2026-10.csv).

**Provenance.** Almost every number is **measured**, taken from the per-stage
logs (`/home/ubuntu/crma-daily-10*.log`, `/home/ubuntu/d-2026*-*.log`,
`crma/logs/d-2026101*-*.log` on the gateway). Numbers marked **[est]** are
estimates. The day-by-day narrative is in the crma repo, branch
`crma-one-scale-one-trigger` @ `4fb83cd`: `RUNBOOK_20261001.md` and
`RUNBOOK_V4_202610{02..15}.md`. From 10-16 on, `daily_run.sh` writes a step
ledger and `make_run_page.py` generates each day's page from it. The steps themselves are in [`../03_DAILY_OPERATIONS.md`](../03_DAILY_OPERATIONS.md).

## What the fifteen days say

1. **24 readers is a robustness setting, not a throughput setting.** At 80–96
   readers a quiet archive realizes a date in 640–750 s. A busy same-day
   archive stalls completely. At 24 readers every day since 10-05 completed,
   but each takes 2,350–3,800 s, which is 4–5× slower. Nobody has yet measured
   24 readers on a quiet day, or anything between 24 and 80. See
   [`../05_BOTTLENECKS_AND_ISSUES.md`](../05_BOTTLENECKS_AND_ISSUES.md) §2.
2. **The source sets the pace, not the cluster.** Pars time, probe success and
   stage ③ retries all degrade together on 10-11 and 10-12, and all three read
   from `s3://ecmwf-forecasts`. The cluster was identical on each day.
3. **The throttle probe is a go/no-go signal, not a predictor.** A 6/10 probe
   preceded a normal day (10-07), and a 10/10 probe still had 22 retries (10-10).
4. **Mirror ② is listing-bound.** Its upload takes seconds, while listing about
   425 k objects takes minutes. An incremental listing would save about
   9 min/day.
5. **Most lost hours were operational, not computational.** They went to
   tokens (10-04, 10-05, 10-08, 10-09), disk (10-06), idle time (10-07) and a
   script bug (10-11). The refresh-token credential, in use from 10-13,
   removes the largest of these.
6. **The 10-11 → 10-13 degradation was transient.** It cleared with no
   configuration change: 974 retries on 10-12, 15 on 10-15 at throttle 9/10.
   Stage ③ time tracked the throttle probe across the episode.
